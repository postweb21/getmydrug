getmydrug workflow

A patient arrives from an ad link or a prescription launch, picks an insurer and a drug, and leaves with approval criteria, cost options and a support link.

Entry point Patient choice Output to the patient Data source

Paying cash skips approval

With no insurance there is no prior authorization, so the flow goes straight from drug choice to cost data and the support link.

Insurers differ by drug

UnitedHealthcare has criteria for all three drugs. Cigna has Nurtec ODT only for now, so the other two show as coming soon.

Answers stay in the tab

The optional interview is scored in the browser. Nothing is sent or stored, and the page only reads static data files.
